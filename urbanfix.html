Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, warranty, and process.

How long does a typical repair take? ▼
Most phone repairs are completed within 1-2 hours. Complex repairs like water damage or motherboard issues may take 24-48 hours. We'll always give you an accurate timeframe before starting the repair.
Do you offer warranty on repairs? ▼
Yes! All our repairs come with a warranty period (typically 3-6 months depending on the repair type). If you experience any issues related to the repair within this period, we'll fix it free of charge.
What areas do you service? ▼
We primarily serve Mbombela/Nelspruit, White River, Hazyview, Nkomazi, Barberton, Middelburg, Witbank/eMalahleni, and surrounding areas in Mpumalanga. Contact us if you're outside these areas.
Are your parts original/OEM? ▼
We use high-quality parts that meet or exceed OEM standards. For certain repairs, we can offer both original and premium compatible options at different price points. We'll always inform you of what we're using.
What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept cash, EFT, and card payments. Payment is typically required upon completion of the repair, though we may require a deposit for certain services or parts ordering.
