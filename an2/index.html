FAQ

Your questions, answered.

Common questions about products, delivery coverage, response times, and the Mbombela warehouse location. Can't find what you're looking for? Get in touch directly.

Contact Us
What does AN Wholesaler supply?
We supply everyday retail categories including groceries, beverages, household products, and personal care ranges for business buyers.
Which brands do you carry?
We work with a trusted supplier base that includes major South African FMCG manufacturers, distributors, and category leaders across everyday retail lines — including Brenner Brands, Diplomat SA, Kingsley Beverages, National Brands Limited, PepsiCo SA, RCL Foods, and Tiger Brands.
Which areas do you deliver to?
The business is based in Mbombela (Nelspruit) and serves buyers across Mpumalanga, with scope to support broader regional delivery routes.
Where is AN Wholesalers and Retail CC t/a OB Distributors based?
The warehouse is located at SHOP 1, 5 Petroleum St, Mbombela, 1214, making it easy for local buyers to find the business and request directions.
How quickly do you respond?
The intended standard is a business-day response within 24 hours for quote and account enquiries. Our operating hours are Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00 SAST.
What is the minimum order value?
Our recommended opening order starts from R6,000. This pallet-ready wholesale fulfillment is designed to be accessible to retailers, wholesalers, and spaza shops of all sizes.
How do I place an order?
Simply reach out via WhatsApp (+27 62 525 4796), phone (013 794 0150), or our enquiry form. We'll provide a clear quote, and once confirmed, your pallet-ready order will be dispatched according to our delivery schedule.
